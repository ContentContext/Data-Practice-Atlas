Data governance and constraints

Provenance. A personal list, expanded with Claude on 19 Nov 2026 in several rounds, plus a Tool category that reproduces Andy Kirk's Visualising Data resources catalogue (293 tools, read the same day) in its seven original sections, without descriptions.

Corrections. On the Visualising Data catalogue page about a dozen links sit on the wrong tile (for example Vega and Vega-Lite, Plotly and Dash); the Atlas corrects them and says so on each card. RightsCon 2026 (Lusaka) was cancelled. "Super dot" is Superdot (Basel); its Venice work is listed as the Come ViVi Venezia project.

How the pictures are made. Treemap groups use each entry's first theme. The decade chart only counts entries with a year. Network positions are computed, not drawn by hand: each theme sits at the average position of the categories its entries belong to, and each entry sits between its category and its themes. Theme families (the shapes) are an editorial grouping made for this page. Each entry's family is the family of its first theme that belongs to one (regions only when nothing else applies); each family has one shape, one pattern and one colour, used the same way in the network, on the cards (background pattern, corner badge, family line and theme chips) and in the footer wordmark. Card base colour is always the category.

Gaps. Leans towards Europe, North America and English-language work despite deliberate additions from Latin America, Africa, MENA, South and Southeast Asia and Italy. Years and places are blank where not confirmed. Event dates change.

Permitted use. A starting reading, contacts and tools list for teaching, inspiration and programme research. Not for citation without checking each link, claims of completeness, or ranking people or studios.
